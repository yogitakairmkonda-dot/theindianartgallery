The Collector

Yogita S.

Founder & Art Collector — The Indian Art Gallery

The Indian Art Gallery is born from a deep fascination with the quiet richness of the Indian Subcontinent — a place where art is not separate from life but woven into its spiritual and cultural fabric.

In Indian tradition, every motif, every colour and every pattern carries intention. This gallery translates that philosophy into contemporary spaces.

Each artwork is thoughtfully created or curated to reflect a sense of stillness, balance and depth — qualities often lost in modern living. These are not just pieces to fill walls but works that invite pause, reflection and conversation.

Our collections are designed for those who seek more than aesthetics — collectors, designers and individuals who value meaning, craftsmanship and cultural narrative.
